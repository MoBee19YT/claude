ParkScope needs its backend before it can show any parking. Deploy the Cloudflare Worker, then paste its address into this file.

  1. Open worker/index.js and deploy it as a Cloudflare Worker (free plan is plenty).
  2. Copy the Worker address it gives you — it ends in .workers.dev.
  3. In index.html, replace the WORKER_URL value near the top of the script with that address.

Just testing? Add ?api=https://your-worker.workers.dev to this page's address instead.
